How to Play Hyper-Scale

Goal
Reach or exceed the Target Damage (shown at the top right) by building a powerful mathematical curve and executing it across 10 turns.
1. Choose a Parent Function
These are the base shapes: Linear (x), Quadratic (x²), Exponential (2ˣ), Logarithmic, and Rational (1/x).
2. Apply Transformations
Modifier cards stretch, compress, or shift the curve. Stack them to create bigger numbers.
3. Execute the Script
Press the button. A particle travels along your curve from turn 0 to 10 while damage accumulates.
Winning
If your final damage ≥ Target → VICTORY.
If not → SYSTEM DEFEATED. You can reboot and try a new combination.
Continuity Bonus
When the curve is continuous you gain a ×3 multiplier. Smooth curves = higher scores.
