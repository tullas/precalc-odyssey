Rates of change and limits

Average rate as secant slope—and what happens as the interval shrinks.

For f(x) = x², the average rate from x₁ to x₂ is (f(x₂)−f(x₁))/(x₂−x₁). Move x₂ toward x₁ and watch the secant get steeper toward the tangent idea.

Secant slope explorer

Interactive Move x₂

Try it: Keep x₁ fixed at 1. Slide x₂ closer to 1. The yellow secant’s slope is the average rate—heading toward 2 (the instantaneous rate at x=1).

x₁ fixed at 1

As x₂ → 1, the average rate approaches 2—the derivative of x² at x=1. That is the calculus idea, previewed here.
