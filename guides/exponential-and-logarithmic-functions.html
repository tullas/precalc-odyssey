Exponential and logarithmic functions

Growth, decay, and logs as the inverse question: “what exponent?”

An exponential function multiplies by a constant factor over equal input steps—interest, populations, cooling. A logarithm answers the inverse: given the result, what exponent produced it?

Growth and decay shape

Basic model: y = a · bˣ. Here a is a starting value and b is the growth factor per unit of x (b > 1 grows; 0 < b < 1 decays).

Logs as inverse

If y = bˣ, then x = log_b(y). On a graph, inverse functions reflect over the line y = x. The log curve rises slowly and is only defined for positive inputs.

Trap: log of a non-positive number is not defined in the reals. Always check the argument of a log.
