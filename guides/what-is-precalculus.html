What is precalculus?

The bridge between algebra and calculus—functions, graphs, and modeling made solid.

Precalculus is not “calculus early.” Its job is to make functions, graphs, and modeling second nature so limits and derivatives later feel natural.

Typical topics

  • Functions — domain, transformations, composition, inverses
  • Polynomial & rational — zeros, end behavior, asymptotes
  • Exponential & log — growth, decay, solving equations
  • Trigonometry — unit circle, graphs, identities
  • Honors / AP extras — sequences, conics, vectors, polar, parametric, matrices, rates & limits

How it differs from algebra

Algebra focuses on solving for x. Precalculus still does that, but asks more often: what does this graph say? Functions become objects you move, stretch, and compose.

Study loop: Read a short guide → practice the matching lab section → use the worked solution after attempts. That builds graph sense, not only answer keys.
