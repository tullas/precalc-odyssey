Trigonometry basics

Angles on the unit circle, sine and cosine as coordinates, and the wave graph—with accessible interactive diagrams.

Trigonometry begins with angles and right triangles, then expands to the unit circle: every angle places a point (cos θ, sin θ) on a circle of radius 1.

You can control the angle with the slider (keyboard-friendly), preset buttons, or by dragging on the diagrams.

Unit circle

Interactive Slider · keyboard · drag

Try it: Use the angle slider or Tab to the presets. Cosine is the horizontal dashed leg; sine is the vertical one. Drag is optional.

What the diagram is showing

  • Point on the circle — position at angle θ
  • Horizontal dashed leg — cosine (x-coordinate)
  • Vertical dashed leg — sine (y-coordinate)
  • Arc — measure of θ from the positive x-axis

Meaning is not color-only: position, dash style, and the numeric readout all convey the same information.

From circle to wave

The height of the point is sin θ. The wave below plots that height against θ. Use the same slider—wave and circle stay linked.
