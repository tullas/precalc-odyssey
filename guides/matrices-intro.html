Matrices: tables that transform

Organized numbers that take a point in and send a new point out.

What a matrix is for

A matrix is a rectangular grid of numbers. In this course the useful picture is a machine: feed it a vector (a point), get a new vector. That is how software scales, rotates, and skews images and game objects.

Where you meet the idea

  • Photo apps — resize and rotate are transformations; under the hood they often use matrices.
  • Games — moving cameras and objects in 2D/3D.
  • Spreadsheets — grids of related numbers (without geometry yet).
  • Science data — mixing components in structured ways.

Scale example

The matrix [[k, 0], [0, k]] multiplies both coordinates by k. Areas scale by k² (the determinant). Try k = 2: sides double, area quadruples.
