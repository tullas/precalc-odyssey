Functions and transformations

How a formula becomes a graph—and how small changes move that graph.

A function assigns one output to each allowed input. Precalculus studies families of functions and how to transform their graphs. The panel below starts from the parent parabola y = x² and lets you change the standard form y = a(x − h)² + k live.

Transformation explorer

Interactive Move the sliders

Try it: Change h (horizontal), k (vertical), and a (stretch / flip). The dashed gray curve is the parent; the blue curve is your transformed graph.

Vertex at (0, 0)
opens up

Trap: In (x − h), positive h moves the graph right. Try h = 2 on the slider and watch the vertex move right.

Domain and range

Domain = allowed inputs. Range = possible outputs. For a parabola opening up with vertex k, the range is y ≥ k.
